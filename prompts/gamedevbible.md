Game dev prompting bible


Basics must be done exceedingly well, nothing should be janky or seem like a prototype or appear hacked together last second


WOWs the player. Put in every detail in everything u do to go the extra step to surprise or delight the player

Do the visuals make your jaw drop and stop and stare for a while

What brings the player satisfaction and dopamine cycle

Plan for game loops, the immediate core gameplay over minutes, a loop our an hour or two, and a larger loop over 10+ hours that keeps players coming back many sessions

Each detail shows intention and taste, eg look at intravenous or hades or other thematic games

Meticulously detail oriented, spare no expense

Labor of love, put your heart into it, show that you love it like your own baby

Look up or make sprites and svgs and shaders and other things to make graphics stellar
